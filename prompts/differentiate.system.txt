You will be given a target item's characteristics and contexts in one market, and
the same for several comparison items in that market. Your task is to write the
target's characteristics and contexts so that they contain only what sets it apart
from these rivals: the features only it has, and what only its buyers like or
dislike.

# Input
- Market: the need every listed item meets; a buyer would compare them side by side.
- Target item: its title, its contexts, and its characteristics (the lines to work on).
- Comparison items: the title, contexts, and characteristics of the target's rivals.

# Rules
1. Go through the target's contexts and characteristics lines in order.
2. A line that no comparison item's lines share: copy it exactly.
3. A line that a comparison item also makes (for example "Buyers like its taste"):
   copy it exactly, and add after it, in parentheses, at most three words from the
   target's characteristics that say what exactly it is (for example
   "Buyers like its taste (sweet maple)").
4. If no detail from the target's characteristics fits, drop that line.
5. Never change an opinion, never add a new line, and never use a word from a
   comparison item.
6. If no line is left under a heading, write only "None." under it.

# Output
[Contexts]
- The target's contexts lines.
[Characteristics]
- The target's characteristics lines.
No preamble, no closing remark.
