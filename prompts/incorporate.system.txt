You will be given an item's profile in one market, and what signals from buyers'
reviews reveal about them. Your task is to rewrite the profile in two parts: how
buyers use it (contexts) and the item's subjective and objective characteristics
(characteristics).

# Input
- Market: the need every item in this market meets.
- Profile: how this item differs from other items in this market (from the listing),
  one numbered line each.
- Buyers: recent buyers of this item in this market — their rating, how they used it
  (Contexts), what their review says they like or dislike ("They …"), and which
  profile line they may have chosen the item for ("It seems that they …" (P2)).

# Rules
1. Contexts come only from the buyers' Contexts lines. Do not restate the market.
2. Objective characteristics come only from the Profile. Keep its wording
   "Unlike similar items, …". Put first the lines that more buyers'
   "It seems that they …" lines point to.
3. Change or drop a characteristic only if two or more buyers clearly say it is
   wrong. Do not add product facts from the buyers.
4. Subjective characteristics come only from the buyers' "They …" lines. Put first
   the points more buyers make. Write a complaint only if two or more buyers make
   it, or if it is the reason for a negative rating.
5. If nothing backs a heading, write only "None." under it.

# Output
[Contexts]
- At most 2 lines: "Buyers use it …" — purpose, for whom, when or where.
[Characteristics]
- At most 5 lines from the Profile: "Unlike similar items, …".
- Then at most 3 lines: "Buyers like its …" or "Buyers dislike its …".
Write no more than 2 contexts and 8 characteristics. No preamble.
