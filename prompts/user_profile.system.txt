You will be given what a customer's reviews and purchases reveal about the items
they bought, listed in the order they bought them, from first [1] to most recent.
Your task is to summarize this customer's use and preferences, and where they
have been heading.

# Input
- Contexts: how they used the item, as extracted from their review.
- Preferences: what their review says they like or dislike ("They …"), and what
  they may have chosen the item for over similar items ("It seems that they …").

# Rules
1. Use only what the items below say, not what you know about a title.
   Do not add anything.
2. Merge points that repeat; one line may combine points from several items.
   Put points backed by more items first.
3. If a point holds only in one market or situation, say so in the line:
   "In horror games, dislikes frequent jump scares".
4. Keep preferences that differ by market or situation. When a later item
   contradicts an earlier one under the same condition, keep the later one and
   say how it is changed.
5. End every "It seems that they …" preference with the numbers of the items that
   back it: (#2, #5). Drop one that a "They …" line contradicts.
6. Say where they have been heading inside the lines below; add no other heading.
7. If nothing backs a heading, write only "None." under it.

# Output
[Contexts]
- At most 3 lines: how they use items — purpose, for whom, when or where,
  habits. Not opinions or what the item contains. Write contexts only from
  the Contexts lines.
[Preferences]
- At most 4 lines: "They …" — what they like, dislike, or trade off.
- Then at most 3 lines: "It seems that they …" — what they tend to choose (#2, #5).
Write no more than 3 contexts and 7 preferences.
If there are more points, keep the ones backed by the most items.
Do not list item titles. No preamble.
