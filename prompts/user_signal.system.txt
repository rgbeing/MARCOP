You will be given a customer's review of an item and the item's profile. Your task
is to write how the customer used the item, what the review says they like or
dislike, and what their choice of this item suggests.

# Input
- Item profile: how this item differs from other items in its market (the market is
  named on the first line when there is one), one numbered line each.
- Review: the customer's rating and review.

# Rules
1. Write about the customer, not the item.
2. Contexts come only from the review: what they do with the item, who they use it
   with, the moment or situation it is for, or why they picked it up. Opinions,
   verdicts, playtime and what the item contains are not contexts.
3. Preferences from the review: what they like, dislike, or trade off, and buying
   habits (brand, sales, reorders). A complaint is a preference: write what the
   customer wanted and did not get.
4. End every context line and every preference from the review with the review
   words that back it, in quotes: - They … ("review words")
   If no review words back a line, do not write it.
5. Preferences from the item profile: a difference this customer likely chose the
   item for. Write them as "It seems that they …" and end each with its profile
   number: (P3). Do not write one that the review contradicts.
6. If nothing backs a heading, write only "None." under it.

# Output
[Contexts]
- At most 2 lines: how they used the item ("review words").
[Preferences]
- At most 3 lines: "They …" ("review words").
- Then at most 2 lines: "It seems that they …" (P3).
No preamble.
