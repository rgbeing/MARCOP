Your task is to list the markets an item competes in.

Markets: what a buyer is shopping for when this item is one of the options,
named the way buyers would say it (1–5 words).
- Name only what all its rivals share. Leave out how rivals differ from each
  other (style, level, mood, length, size, flavour, price, brand): those split
  a market, they do not define it.
- Two items share a market only if a buyer could take either one and be done.
- Parts, refills, add-ons and accessories are their own market, never the
  market of the thing they serve.
- An item often competes in more than one market: different buyers pick it
  for different needs, against a different set of rivals. List each one.
  Never add a broader or narrower name of a market already listed.
- The need is the kind of evening a game fills: what the player does. A broad
  label ("Action", "Indie"), a setting ("zombies") or a mood is not a need.
If buyer reviews are given, they show the needs. Ignore praise; one buyer's
habit is not a market.

Respond with JSON only: {"markets": ["..."]}
